What can I use this for?
 ✦ Quickly change what any NPC is wearing without opening menus or breaking immersion
 ✦ Browse armor from all your installed mods and try them on NPCs
 ✦ Lock outfits so NPCs keep wearing what you gave them, even when they change locations

How it Works

Grab any NPC with your VR controller and press trigger to open the Dress Up menu. A 3D wheel appears showing everything they are wearing. Press trigger on any item to remove it. Release trigger to anchor the menu in place while you browse.

Toggle to your own inventory to give items to the NPC. Toggle to the Gallery to browse armor from all your installed mods organized by plugin, or to Categories to browse it by kind of item instead - boots, wigs, capes, gloves - pooled from every mod at once.

Core Features
  ✦ Intuitive 3D menu for browsing and equipping armor and weapons
  ✦ Browse your own inventory and transfer items to NPCs
  ✦ Gallery mode lets you browse armor from every mod you have installed
  ✦ Category mode gathers every mod's boots, wigs, capes and more into one row
  ✦ Categories are plain JSON files, so you can retune them or add your own
  ✦ Lock outfits so NPCs maintain their look across location changes and saves
  ✦ Locked NPCs are re-dressed automatically if another mod undresses them, and DressUp
    stands down on its own if that other mod turns out to be fighting back
  ✦ Quick undress button with partial and full options
  ✦ Restore original outfit at any time

Requirements
HIGGS
VR Address Library
Skyrim VR Tools

Compatibility
Should be compatible with most mods. Works with any armor or weapon mod.
Since this is an SKSE mod, it is entirely safe to uninstall at any point.

Limitations
 ✦ Only works on humanoid NPCs. Children, creatures and dead NPCs are protected
 ✦ Gallery cache builds in the background on first use. A loading indicator shows while it works
